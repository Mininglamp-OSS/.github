## About

Mininglamp-OSS is the open-source home for **Octo** — an AI-native team communication platform where humans and AI Agents collaborate as equals.

Octo reimagines how teams work together: AI Agents aren't hidden behind personal terminals — they join your channels, participate in discussions, and execute tasks alongside your team. Your taste, judgment, and context stay yours. The AI handles the thinking and doing; you focus on what matters most — the decisions only you can make.

### Core Principles

- **Open & Self-hostable** — Code is open, data stays yours. Deploy on your infrastructure, keep full control.
- **Context belongs to you** — Your work context, your implicit knowledge, your decision patterns are protected at the highest level.
- **Taste is irreplaceable** — AI gets better at reasoning and executing. Your judgment and taste are what can't be replicated.
- **Orchestration at scale** — Humans, Agents, and tools coordinated through a single collaboration layer.
